Privacy Policy

Store respects your privacy and is designed to help you manage your business records.

Information Stored by the App

Store allows users to enter and maintain business records, including information such as products, prices, weights, totals and dates.

Business records are stored locally on the user's device.

Google Account and Google Drive

Store provides an optional backup and restore feature using Google Drive.

When a user chooses to use this feature, the app uses the user's Google account to access the application's private Google Drive storage area for storing and restoring backup data.

Store does not sell or share your business records with third parties.

Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is used to authenticate the user and provide access to the Google Drive backup feature.

Data Security

The app uses Google's authentication and authorization mechanisms when accessing Google Drive.

Data Deletion

Users can remove their locally stored business data through the application. Google Drive backup data can also be removed from the user's Google Drive application data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Store application, please contact the application developer.
