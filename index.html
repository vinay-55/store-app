Store

Store is a simple business management application designed to help small businesses maintain their daily business records.

About Store

Store helps users record and manage business information such as products, prices, weights, totals and daily records.

The application stores business data locally on the user's device and provides an optional Google Drive backup and restore feature.

Features

• Business record management
• Daily record keeping
• Local data storage
• Manual backup
• Automatic backup
• Data restore
